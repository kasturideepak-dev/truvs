Case Study 05 · Process Modernization · Business Validation & Testing

Celonis Migration & UAT Leadership — Supply Chain Transformation

400 processes rebuilt. 712 bugs found and fixed. 100% UAT pass rate achieved.

~400 Processes Rebuilt in Celonis
712 Bugs Found & 100% Fixed
100% UAT Test Case Pass Rate
0 Data Quality Errors on Baseline
Client profile Fortune 50 hyperscaler · Multi-billion-dollar cloud infrastructure · Ongoing · Global Technology Company — CSCP
Challenge & Approach

Challenge & context

~400 supply chain processes were locked in a legacy BPM system being retired — with poor data quality: missing owners, incomplete mandatory fields, and Celonis parameters absent.

Engineering releases were constrained by insufficient internal UAT leadership — critical defects were not being caught before production.

TruVs approach

Workstream A — Process Modernization: Rebuilt the full BPM process portfolio natively in Celonis (not copy-paste) across two quarters, correcting data quality on every migrated process. Introduced weekly quality-check protocols now run independently by the client.

Workstream B — UAT Leadership: Embedded TruVs UAT leads across multiple 8-week cycles. Full lifecycle owned: requirements deep-dive, scenario design, execution coordination, defect tracking, and closure sign-off.

Achieved zero data-quality errors on the migrated Celonis baseline by January 2026, creating the foundation for the client's planned process knowledge database and AI agent.

Outcomes
  • ~400 processes rebuilt natively in Celonis — foundation for process mining and AI agent now viable
  • Zero data-quality errors on the migrated baseline; weekly quality discipline institutionalized
  • 712 bugs found and 100% resolved across completed UAT cycles
  • 100% test case execution and pass rate across every completed cycle
  • 10+ Severity-1 defects caught pre-release in a single 8-week CapEx cycle
  • Client has a usable Celonis baseline for the first time; process mining now unblocked
Key insight

'Doing it right the first time' on the Celonis migration — native rebuild vs. copy-paste — avoided the multi-pass rework that similar programs typically require. Quality discipline introduced by TruVs is now a repeatable client-run process.
